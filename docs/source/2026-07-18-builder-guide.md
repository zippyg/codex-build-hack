## Build + submission

> **Teams:** up to 4 people. Every team member must be individually registered and approved.
>
> **Build:** start something new or push an existing project further with Codex. A new project, open-source extension, developer tool or agent workflow are all in scope.
>
> **Submit:** the organisers will explain the submission flow at 10:40. Your entry must be in by **17:00**.
>
> **Be demo-ready:** have a working product, a clear problem/value statement and the links needed to open it quickly.

## What judges score

- **Idea + usefulness** - a problem worth solving and a compelling solution.
- **Use of Codex** - meaningful use of Codex in how the project was built.
- **Execution** - the project works and the implementation is thoughtful.
- **Demo + clarity** - the value is obvious and demonstrated well.

Round 1 reviews every submission; each receives at least two scores. The top six advance. The final demo block is **20 minutes total**, so finalists should expect a very short, tightly timed demo.
