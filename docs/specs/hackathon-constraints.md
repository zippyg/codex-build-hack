# Hackathon constraints

This is the execution brief derived from the full published guide in [the source record](../source/2026-07-18-builder-guide.md).

## Non-negotiables

- Local submission is due at 17:00 BST on 18 July 2026.
- Global Build Week entry is a distinct Devpost submission, due 17:00 PT on 21 July 2026, or 01:00 BST on 22 July.
- Teams have at most four people and every member must be individually registered and approved.
- A Devpost submission does not enter the London competition. A London submission does not enter the global challenge.
- The project can be new or an existing project pushed forward with Codex.

## Win condition

The project should make its problem, utility, Codex contribution, working execution and value obvious within a two-minute demo. Top six reach a final round with up to one additional minute for questions.

## Operating timeline

- 10:40: capture the organiser's exact submission flow and any changes to the published rules.
- 10:50-13:00: prove the critical user journey.
- 13:30-16:15: make the demo reliable and prepare the submission materials.
- 16:15-16:45: test the demo from a fresh starting point and prepare every required link.
- 16:45-17:00: submit locally. Do not spend this window coding.
- After the local submission: separately complete the Devpost entry if entering the global challenge.
